CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Please have the restaurant owner review and complete this notice before accepting real customer orders.

Information used for an order

When you submit an order, the restaurant receives your name, phone number, delivery address, order items, and any notes you provide. This information is stored on the restaurant's server so authorized staff can review and handle the order.

WhatsApp

After submission, you may choose to open WhatsApp with a prepared order message. The message is not sent until you press Send. WhatsApp handles messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

Payments and retention

This app does not collect card or payment details. Orders and contact information remain in the private restaurant dashboard until authorized staff delete them. Contact the restaurant to ask about your information or request its deletion.

Before the restaurant launches

The restaurant owner must replace this demo branding with the correct business name and contact details, set a customer-data retention schedule, and confirm this notice meets the privacy rules that apply to the business. This template is not legal advice.
